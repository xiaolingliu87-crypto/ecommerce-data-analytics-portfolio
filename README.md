# E-commerce Data Analytics Portfolio
This repository showcases two independent e-commerce data analytics projects focused on customer behavior, customer value, and product and brand performance.

Using Python and data analytics techniques, these projects explore customer segmentation and retention, acquisition performance, product conversion, brand performance, and pricing effectiveness to generate business-oriented insights.
## Project 1: E-commerce Customer Analytics: Segmentation, Retention & Customer Lifetime Value
**Objective:** Analyze customer behavior and purchasing patterns to understand customer value, retention, and acquisition performance.
### Key Analyses
- **Data Preparation & Quality Assessment:** Cleaned and validated customer, transaction, and event datasets, addressing missing values, data consistency, and analytical data quality.
- **RFM Analysis & Customer Segmentation:** Applied Recency, Frequency, and Monetary analysis and K-Means clustering to identify customer segments with different purchasing behaviors and value profiles.
- **Cohort & Retention Analysis:** Analyzed customer retention across acquisition cohorts to identify retention patterns over time.
- **Customer Lifetime Value (CLV):** Evaluated historical customer value and purchasing patterns to understand differences in customer contribution.
- **Acquisition & Campaign Analysis:** Compared customer acquisition channels and campaign performance to examine customer acquisition and engagement patterns.
- **Customer Funnel Analysis:** Evaluated customer reach and conversion across funnel stages, including channel-level engagement and bounce behavior.
### Tools & Techniques
Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Scikit-learn, RFM Analysis, K-Means Clustering, Cohort Analysis, Funnel Analysis
## Project 2: E-commerce Product & Brand Performance Analytics
**Objective:** Evaluate product, category, and brand performance and examine pricing and discount patterns to identify business performance drivers and optimization opportunities.
### Key Analyses
- **Product & Category Performance:** Evaluated category-level GMV, order volume, and sales contribution to understand product portfolio performance.
- **Product Conversion Funnel:** Analyzed customer progression through View → Click → Add to Cart → Purchase, using unique customers at each stage to assess conversion and drop-off patterns.
- **Brand Performance Analysis:** Compared brands across GMV, order volume, average order value (AOV), conversion rate, and refund rate to assess brand-level performance.
- **Premium Product Analysis:** Compared premium and non-premium products in terms of GMV contribution, AOV, conversion, and refund performance.
- **Pricing & Discount Effectiveness:** Examined differences between discounted and non-discounted orders, including order value, repeat purchasing behavior, and historical customer revenue.
- **CLV Prediction:** Explored machine learning approaches, including Logistic Regression and XGBoost, to estimate future customer value and evaluated model performance.
### Tools & Techniques
Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Scikit-learn, XGBoost, Funnel Analysis, Pricing Analysis, Performance Metrics, Regression Modeling
## Technical Skills
- **Programming:** Python
- **Data Manipulation:** Pandas, NumPy
- **Data Visualization:** Matplotlib, Seaborn
- **Machine Learning:** Scikit-learn, XGBoost, K-Means Clustering, Regression Models
- **Business Analytics:** KPI Development, Customer Segmentation, Cohort & Retention Analysis, Funnel Analysis, CLV Analysis, Product & Brand Performance, Pricing & Discount Analysis
## About This Portfolio
These are independent portfolio projects developed using e-commerce datasets. They demonstrate practical applications of data preparation, exploratory data analysis, customer and product analytics, statistical analysis, and machine learning.

The analyses focus on descriptive insights and business-oriented performance evaluation. Predictive modeling results are evaluated based on model performance rather than assumed predictive effectiveness.
